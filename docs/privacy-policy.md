# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** [DATE]

Paso Robles Food Cooperative ("we," "us," or "the Co-op") operates PRFC Connect to help members refer friends and communicate with contact groups. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

---

## Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you use PRFC Connect:

- **Member information:** Name, email address, phone number (if provided), and member ID from your Co-op account
- **Referral information:** Names and email addresses of prospects you refer
- **Contact group data:** Group membership and communication preferences
- **SMS consent records:** Phone number, consent timestamp, the consent message you agreed to, and IP address
- **Engagement data:** Message delivery status for troubleshooting

---

## How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

- Send referral emails on your behalf
- Deliver messages from contact groups you belong to
- Send SMS messages (only with your explicit consent)
- Track message delivery for troubleshooting
- Maintain compliance with email and SMS regulations

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

---

## Email Communications

You may receive emails from contact group owners or Co-op administrators. Every email includes:

- Clear sender identification
- Our physical mailing address
- A working unsubscribe link

You can unsubscribe from group emails at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link in any message. Unsubscribe requests are processed immediately.

---

## SMS Communications

### Opt-In Required

We only send SMS messages to members who explicitly opt in. Consent is not required for Co-op membership or services.

### Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on group activity. Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier and plan.

### Opt-Out

You can opt out at any time by:

- Replying **STOP** to any message
- Texting **HELP** for assistance
- Contacting the Co-op office

Opt-out requests are processed immediately.

### Quiet Hours

We do not send SMS messages before 9:00 AM or after 8:00 PM Pacific Time.

### One-to-One Consent

Your SMS opt-in applies to communications from Paso Robles Food Cooperative only. Consent does not extend to third-party businesses or affiliates.

---

## Data Sharing

We do not share or sell your information to third parties for marketing purposes.

We may share information with service providers who help deliver messages (such as email and SMS delivery services). These providers are contractually required to protect your information and use it only for service delivery.

---

## Data Retention

| Data Type              | Retention Period                                               |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Member information     | Duration of membership + 3 years                               |
| Referral records       | 3 years                                                        |
| Message history        | 3 years                                                        |
| SMS consent records    | 5 years (TCPA requirement)                                     |
| Email suppression list | Permanent (to prevent sends to bounced or opted-out addresses) |

---

## Compliance Records

As required by TCPA and Campaign Registry guidelines, we maintain records of all opt-ins and opt-outs, including timestamps and method of consent. These records are kept securely and may be used to demonstrate compliance with regulatory requirements.

---

## Data Security

We protect your information using:

- Encrypted connections (HTTPS)
- Secure authentication
- Access controls limiting who can view member data

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. We implement reasonable measures to protect your information.

---

## Your Rights

You have the right to:

- Access your personal information
- Correct inaccurate information
- Opt out of email or SMS communications at any time
- Request deletion of your data (subject to legal retention requirements)

To exercise these rights, contact the Co-op office.

---

## Children's Privacy

PRFC Connect is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Significant changes will be communicated via email or website notification.
